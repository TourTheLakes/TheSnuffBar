About

A nod to Kendal’s past, built for nights now.

Our roots reach deep into Kendal’s past. With a nod to the town’s historic snuff production, you’ll find traces of old craft woven throughout the space — from original snuff press displays, and small handcrafted collections of antique snuff boxes behind the bar. It’s history you can sit with, drink beside, and enjoy — not behind glass, but as part of the atmosphere.

Behind the scenes (and often out front), Rosie is our licensee and resident wine selector — personally choosing every bottle for quality and story. Our drinks menu champions a great range of beers, spirits and wines, including several produced locally — because the best things are often made close to home.

Milly brings the energy, taking care of social media and entertainment, while Lottie is the welcoming face of the bar, front of house and making sure everyone feels at ease the moment they walk in. Together, all three of us are committed to creating a warm, relaxed, and memorable experience — whether you're popping in for a quick drink or settling in for the evening.
