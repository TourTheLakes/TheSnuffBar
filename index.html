About

Our roots reach deep into Kendal’s past. With a nod to the town’s historic snuff production, you’ll find traces of old craft woven throughout the space: a table fashioned from an original snuff press, and a small but characterful collection of antique snuff boxes behind the bar. It’s history you can sit with, drink beside, and enjoy — not behind glass, but as part of the atmosphere.

Behind the scenes (and often out front), Rosie is our licensee and resident wine selector, personally choosing every bottle for quality and genuine value for money. Our drinks menu champions a great range of beers, spirits and wines, including several produced locally — because we believe the best things are often made close to home.

Milly brings the energy, taking care of social media and entertainment, while Lottie is the welcoming face of the bar, front of house and making sure everyone feels at ease the moment they walk in. Together, all three of us are committed to creating a warm, relaxed, memorable experience — whether you’re popping in for a quiet drink or settling in for the evening.

We host a local darts team and have a board available for public use, alongside a wide selection of games for friendly competition. Upstairs, our function room has been home to choirs, meetings, parties and more. It’s available to book for private events, with cold food provided on request.

Above all, our focus is simple: a pleasant experience, an atmospheric setting, and a bar that feels like it belongs to the people who drink in it.

We look forward to welcoming customers old and new.
