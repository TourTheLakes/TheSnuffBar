Old Craft. New Spirit.

Welcome to The Snuff Bar

A Kent Street pub rooted in tradition, craft, and character. The Snuff Bar is a space to drink well, relax, and enjoy good company — not behind glass, but as part of the atmosphere.

About Us

Tucked away on Kent Street, our bar is proudly family run — a labour of love led by mum and her two daughters, with a shared belief that a great bar is about far more than what’s in the glass.

Our roots reach deep into Kendal’s past. With a nod to the town’s historic snuff production, you’ll find traces of old craft woven throughout the space — from original snuff press displays and small handcrafted collections, to antiques and pieces behind the bar. It’s history you can sit with, drink beside, and enjoy.

Behind the scenes, each bottle is carefully selected, personally chosen, and valued for its quality and story. Our drinks menu celebrates regional gins, craft lagers, real ales, and classic pours — because the best things are often made close to home.

The Snuff Bar is a welcoming space for conversation, entertainment, and relaxed evenings. Whether you’re stopping in for a quiet drink or settling in for the evening, the bar belongs to the people who drink in it.

Upcoming Events

We host a local darts team and have a pool table available for public use, alongside a wide selection of board games for friendly competition.

Looking ahead into 2026, we’re excited to introduce regular live music nights, quizzes, games evenings, and more — adding even more reasons to gather, linger, and return.
